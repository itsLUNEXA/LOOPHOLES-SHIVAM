Autonomous Discovery

Powered by TinyFish Web Agents

Finding tech events and hackathons shouldn't require manual syncs or checking dozens of separate social channels. Vibento uses TinyFish-powered discovery to automatically fetch and refresh information from configured organizer feeds, websites, and community schedules.

  • Targeted Source Refresh: Actively checks configured sources for events, hackathons, and competitions.
  • Transparent Attribution: Every imported listing shows its original source and actual check timestamp.
  • Quality Safeguards: Intelligent deduplication and moderation rules protect quality without overwriting community edits.
Note: TinyFish refreshes targeted sources configured by platform administrators. Listings reflect organizer details and are not independently endorsed by Vibento staff.
